轻扫（Sweeply）隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 22 日

轻扫是一款完全在你的 Mac 上本地运行的磁盘清理工具。

  • 不收集任何数据。轻扫不收集、不存储、不上传任何个人信息、文件信息或使用数据。
  • 不联网。轻扫没有网络访问权限，不会连接任何服务器，也不包含广告、统计或第三方 SDK。
  • 文件访问。轻扫只在你授权后读取个人文件夹中的缓存、日志、下载等位置，用于在本机计算可清理的空间。清理操作只会把你确认的项目移到废纸篓。
  • 本地设置。轻扫只在本机保存你的授权记录和勾选偏好，删除应用即可一并清除。

如有疑问，请在 GitHub Issues 中提交问题。

Sweeply Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

Sweeply is a disk cleaner that runs entirely on your Mac.

  • No data collection. Sweeply does not collect, store or upload any personal information, file information or usage data.
  • No network access. Sweeply has no network entitlement, never connects to any server, and contains no ads, analytics or third-party SDKs.
  • File access. After you grant access, Sweeply reads locations such as caches, logs and downloads in your home folder only to calculate reclaimable space on your Mac. Cleaning only moves the items you confirm to the Trash.
  • Local settings. Sweeply keeps your access grant and selection preferences on your Mac only. Deleting the app removes them.

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub Issues.
